Elephants in Easy German

Step into the fascinating world of Elephants (Elefanten), the largest and gentlest giants on land. Did you know that elephants can actually "hear" seismic rumbles through sensitive receptors in their giant feet? Explore the multifunctionality of the trunk with over 40,000 muscles, their tight-knit matriarchal family groups led by experienced grandmothers, and the extraordinary empathy and memory that make them truly unique.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Elephants communicate over miles using deep infrasound that travels through the ground and is felt by their feet.
  • An elephant's trunk contains over 40,000 individual muscles and can lift heavy logs as well as pick a single blade of grass.
  • Herds are led by an older matriarch whose decades of memory guide the family to water during severe droughts.
  • Elephants exhibit deep emotional bonds, mourning deceased family members and greeting friends with joyful trumpeting.
