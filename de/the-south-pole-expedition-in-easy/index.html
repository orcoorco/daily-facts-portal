The South Pole Expedition in Easy German

Journey to the bottom of the world in 1911, where Norwegian explorer Roald Amundsen led a daring, secret expedition to conquer the South Pole. Learn how Amundsen secretly changed his route from the Arctic to Antarctica, relied on indigenous Inuit survival techniques and hardy Greenland sled dogs, and overcame bitter -56°C temperatures and internal team rivalries to plant the Norwegian flag first.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Amundsen kept his plan to reach the South Pole a total secret from his own crew and sponsors until they were already at sea.
  • Unlike British rival Robert Falcon Scott, Amundsen relied exclusively on skis and 52 well-trained Greenland sled dogs.
  • An aborted first attempt in extreme -56°C frost nearly cost lives and led to a bitter clash with explorer Hjalmar Johansen.
  • On December 14, 1911, Amundsen's five-man team became the very first humans to stand at the geographic South Pole.
