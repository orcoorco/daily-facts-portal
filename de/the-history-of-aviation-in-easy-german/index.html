The History of Aviation in Easy German

From paper hot air balloons to supersonic jets, trace the daring history of human flight! Discover how humanity unlocked the dream of soaring through the skies across centuries of experimentation. Follow the journey from the Montgolfier brothers' paper balloon in 1783 and Otto Lilienthal's graceful gliders to the Wright brothers' historic powered flight at Kitty Hawk in 1903.

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • The Montgolfier brothers made the first successful hot air balloon flight in 1783 using paper and linen.
  • Otto Lilienthal proved the principles of aerodynamics with over 2,000 successful glider flights in Germany.
  • The Wright brothers achieved the first controlled, powered airplane flight in December 1903.
  • Aviation advanced from fragile wood and cloth biplanes to commercial passenger jets in just a few decades.
