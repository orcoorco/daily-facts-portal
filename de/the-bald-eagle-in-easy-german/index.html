The Bald Eagle in Easy German

Soar into the skies with the Bald Eagle (Weißkopfseeadler), the majestic national bird of the United States! Discover how these incredible birds build the largest tree nests in the animal kingdom—weighing as much as a small car. Learn about their dramatic "cartwheel display" mating ritual high above the ground, how chemical pesticides like DDT nearly drove them to extinction, and how strict conservation helped their population stage a triumphant recovery.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Bald eagles build giant tree nests up to 4 meters deep and weighing over two tons.
  • During courtship, eagle pairs lock talons in midair and free-fall spinning toward the earth before pulling apart.
  • The widespread use of pesticide DDT made their eggshells brittle, causing numbers to crash in the 20th century.
  • Protected by federal law and environmental bans, bald eagles have made one of history's greatest wildlife recoveries.
