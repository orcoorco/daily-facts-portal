The Alpine Ibex in Easy German

Discover the dramatic rescue of the Alpine Ibex (Alpensteinbock), one of Europe's greatest conservation triumphs! By 1820, this majestic mountain goat was hunted down to just ~100 individuals in Italy's Gran Paradiso. Learn how King Victor Emmanuel II created a royal hunting reserve to save the species, and how audacious Swiss gamekeepers smuggled live ibex kids across the Alps in 1906 to bring the species roaring back to Switzerland.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Uncontrolled hunting for meat, horns, and folk medicine reduced the entire world population of ibex to around 100 in Italy.
  • Italian King Victor Emmanuel II declared Gran Paradiso a royal reserve in 1821, saving the last herd from extinction.
  • When Italy refused to sell ibex to Switzerland, Swiss gamekeepers hired poachers to smuggle young ibex across the border in 1906.
  • Today, over 50,000 Alpine ibex thrive across the Alps in Switzerland, Italy, Austria, France, and Germany.
