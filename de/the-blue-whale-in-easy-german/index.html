The Blue Whale in Easy German

Plunge into the deep ocean to meet the largest animal ever known to have lived on Earth: the colossal blue whale! Stretching up to 30 meters long and weighing up to 200 tons, this gentle giant dwarfs even the largest dinosaurs. Discover how its car-sized heart pumps thousands of liters of blood, how it filters tons of tiny krill each day through baleen plates, and how its low-frequency songs travel across entire oceans.

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Blue whales can reach lengths of 30 meters (100 feet) and weigh up to 200 tons.
  • A blue whale's heart weighs around 180 kg (400 lbs) and beats as slowly as 2 times per minute during deep dives.
  • Despite their immense size, they feed almost exclusively on tiny shrimp-like krill, eating up to 4 tons per day.
  • Their vocalizations can reach 188 decibels, allowing them to communicate across hundreds of miles of ocean.
