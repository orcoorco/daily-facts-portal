The British Empire in Easy German

Examine the rise, reach, and profound global legacy of the British Empire, the largest empire in human history. At its peak in the early 20th century, it spanned nearly a quarter of Earth's landmass and population. Explore the economic machinery of the East India Company, the sugar plantations of the Caribbean, the spread of the English language, and the complex movements for independence and decolonization.

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • At its height, the British Empire ruled over approximately 450 million people—one fifth of humanity.
  • The phrase 'the empire on which the sun never sets' was coined because it had territories in every global time zone.
  • Maritime naval power and global trade networks like the East India Company drove its imperial expansion.
  • Decolonization accelerated rapidly following World War II, culminating in the modern Commonwealth of Nations.
