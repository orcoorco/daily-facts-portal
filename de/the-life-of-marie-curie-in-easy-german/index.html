The Life of Marie Curie in Easy German

Celebrate the inspiring life and scientific brilliance of Marie Curie, one of history's greatest pioneers. Born in Warsaw, she overcame poverty and discrimination to study at the Sorbonne in Paris. Discover how her tireless research into uranium rays led to the discovery of two new chemical elements—polonium and radium—and revolutionized physics and medicine, earning her two Nobel Prizes.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Marie Curie was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize and the only person to win in two different scientific fields.
  • She discovered two radioactive elements: Polonium (named after her homeland Poland) and Radium.
  • She coined the term 'radioactivity' to describe the spontaneous emission of energy from atoms.
  • During World War I, she developed mobile X-ray units ('Petites Curies') to help wounded soldiers on the front.
