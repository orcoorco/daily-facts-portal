The Voyage of Charles Darwin in Easy German

Board the HMS Beagle alongside young naturalist Charles Darwin on his historic five-year voyage around the world from 1831 to 1836. Explore how Darwin's observations of unique finches and giant tortoises on the isolated Galápagos Islands sparked the revolutionary idea of evolution by natural selection.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Darwin set sail on the HMS Beagle in 1831 as an unpaid 22-year-old naturalist.
  • The five-year journey surveyed South America, the Galápagos Islands, Australia, and South Africa.
  • Darwin noticed that finches on different islands had beaks adapted to their specific food sources.
  • His observations formed the foundation of his landmark 1859 book 'On the Origin of Species'.
