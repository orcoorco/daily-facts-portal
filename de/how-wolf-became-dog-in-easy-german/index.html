How Wolf became Dog in Easy German

Travel back 30,000 years into the Ice Age to uncover how wild, fierce wolves transformed into loyal domestic dogs sleeping by our fires. Discover how brave wolves began scavenging leftover food near human hunter-gatherer camps, leading to an extraordinary partnership where both species protected and hunted together.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Dogs were the very first animals domesticated by humans, over 20,000 to 30,000 years ago.
  • Less fearful wolves benefited from leftover meat near human camps, starting a gradual taming process.
  • Cooperation in hunting and guarding created an evolutionary bond between humans and canines.
  • Over generations, physical changes appeared: smaller teeth, floppier ears, and expressive facial muscles.
