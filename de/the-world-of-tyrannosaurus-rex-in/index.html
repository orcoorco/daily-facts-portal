Enter the prehistoric world of Tyrannosaurus Rex, the undisputed king of the Late Cretaceous dinosaurs! Towering 4 meters tall and weighing up to 9 tons, this apex predator ruled North America 66 million years ago. Uncover fascinating scientific facts about T-Rex's bone-crushing bite force, binocular vision, keen sense of smell, and the ongoing debate over whether it was an active hunter or opportunistic scavenger.

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • T-Rex lived approximately 68 to 66 million years ago in western North America.
  • It possessed the strongest bite force of any known terrestrial animal—capable of crushing solid bone.
  • Its forward-facing eyes provided binocular depth perception superior to modern hawks.
  • Despite small two-fingered arms, its legs and muscular tail allowed steady movement across open terrain.
