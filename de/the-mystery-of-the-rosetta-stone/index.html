The Mystery of the Rosetta Stone in Easy German

Uncover the story of the stone slab that unlocked ancient Egyptian history! In July 1799, French soldiers rebuilding a fort in the Nile Delta city of Rashid discovered the mysterious Rosetta Stone. Learn how this granodiorite slab, carved with the same decree in Egyptian Hieroglyphs, Demotic, and Ancient Greek, allowed French linguist Jean-François Champollion to crack the hieroglyphic code in 1822.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • The Rosetta Stone was discovered in 1799 by French soldiers during Napoleon's Egyptian expedition.
  • It features a decree issued in 196 BC written in three scripts: Hieroglyphs, Demotic, and Ancient Greek.
  • Because scholars could read Greek, the stone served as a bilingual key to deciphering lost hieroglyphs.
  • Jean-François Champollion finally cracked the phonetic system of hieroglyphs in 1822 after years of study.
