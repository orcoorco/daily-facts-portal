The Tasmanian Tiger in Easy German

Discover the bittersweet story of the Tasmanian Tiger (Thylacine), the unique striped marsupial that once roamed Tasmania. Despite its dog-like appearance and tiger-striped back, it carried its young in a pouch. Learn how misunderstandings by European sheep farmers led to government bounties that pushed the species to extinction in 1936, and explore modern scientific efforts attempting to resurrect this lost icon.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • The Tasmanian Tiger was not a dog or cat, but a carnivorous marsupial with a pouch like a kangaroo.
  • It had distinctive dark stripes across its lower back and could open its jaws to an astonishing 80 degrees.
  • Bounty hunting, habitat loss, and disease caused its rapid decline during the 19th and early 20th centuries.
  • The last known individual, 'Benjamin', died at Hobart Zoo in September 1936, just 59 days after receiving legal protection.
