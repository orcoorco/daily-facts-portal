The Solar System in Easy German

Embark on an epic cosmic tour of our home in space: the Solar System (Sonnensystem)! Born 4.6 billion years ago from a swirling cloud of gas and dust, our cosmic neighborhood stretches far beyond what the eye can see. Explore the four rocky inner worlds, the crushing pressures inside gas giant Jupiter, ringed Saturn, and the icy frontiers of the Kuiper Belt and Oort Cloud where comets are born.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Our solar system formed roughly 4.6 billion years ago from the gravitational collapse of a giant molecular dust cloud.
  • The Sun holds over 99.8% of all the mass in the entire solar system.
  • Jupiter's atmosphere has atmospheric pressures so extreme that any entering spacecraft is crushed within minutes.
  • Beyond Neptune lies the Kuiper Belt, home to dwarf planet Pluto and millions of icy celestial bodies.
