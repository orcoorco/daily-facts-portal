The Lion in Easy German

Meet the legendary "King of the Jungle," the Lion (Löwe)! Lions are unique among big cats for living in complex social prides where cooperative lionesses do the majority of the hunting across the open savanna. Explore why dark, heavy manes signal health and testosterone to rivals, how lions once roamed southern Europe and North America in prehistoric times, and discover the mysterious horny spur hidden in the tip of a lion's tail.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Lions are the only truly social wild cat species, living in family prides containing up to several dozen related members.
  • A male lion's dark mane is an honest signal of strength and high testosterone, but causes significant heat stress in summer.
  • Until recent millennia, extinct lion species and subspecies roamed widely across Europe, Asia, and North America.
  • A lion's tail ends in a dark tuft of hair concealing a small, mysterious horny claw-like spur.
