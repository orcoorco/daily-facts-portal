The Tomb of Tutankhamun in Easy German

Step into the Valley of the Kings in November 1922, where British archaeologist Howard Carter made the most sensational discovery in archaeological history: the intact tomb of Pharaoh Tutankhamun. Peep through the doorway to see "wonderful things"—over 5,000 priceless treasures, golden chariots, ornate thrones, and the iconic solid-gold death mask of Egypt's famous boy king.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Tutankhamun became pharaoh of Ancient Egypt around age 9 and ruled until his sudden death at age 19.
  • Howard Carter and Lord Carnarvon spent five years searching before discovering the buried entrance staircase.
  • The tomb contained over 5,000 undisturbed artifacts, including three nesting coffins and the solid gold mask.
  • The discovery captivated global media and popularized the famous legend of the 'Curse of the Pharaohs'.
