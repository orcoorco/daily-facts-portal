The Journey of the Golden Discs in Easy German

Venture into deep interstellar space aboard NASA's twin Voyager spacecraft. Launched in 1977, Voyager 1 and 2 carry iconic gold-plated copper phonograph records as messages to extraterrestrial civilizations. Explore how astronomer Carl Sagan and his team curated sounds of thunder and birds, musical selections from Bach to Chuck Berry, greetings in 55 languages, and 115 images into an eternal cosmic time capsule.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Voyager 1 and 2 were launched in 1977 to explore Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune before heading into deep space.
  • Each probe carries a 12-inch gold-plated copper disc containing sounds and images of Earth.
  • The records include greetings in 55 ancient and modern languages and 90 minutes of diverse global music.
  • Voyager 1 is now over 24 billion kilometers from Earth—the farthest human-made object in history.
