The Polar Bear in Easy German

Venture into the freezing Arctic ice desert to meet the Polar Bear (Eisbär), the undisputed apex predator of the far north. Beneath their thick, translucent white coat lies jet-black skin designed to absorb solar warmth. Discover how mother polar bears hibernate in snow dens to nurse tiny cubs with milk as rich as whipping cream, how they patiently hunt seals at breathing holes, and why melting sea ice threatens their future.

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • A polar bear's fur is actually transparent and hollow, reflecting light while its underlying skin is completely black.
  • Mother bears fast for months inside snow dens while nursing tiny newborn cubs with milk containing 30% fat.
  • They possess an incredible sense of smell, able to detect a seal under a meter of snow from over a kilometer away.
  • Polar bears are classified as marine mammals because they spend most of their lives on drifting sea ice hunting seals.
