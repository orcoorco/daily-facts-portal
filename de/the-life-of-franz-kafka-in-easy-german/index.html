The Life of Franz Kafka in Easy German

Step into the mysterious double life of Franz Kafka in turn-of-the-century Prague. By day, he was a quiet, diligent insurance clerk stamping documents in a gray office; by night, he wrote groundbreaking masterpieces like "The Metamorphosis" (Die Verwandlung). In this episode, explore Kafka's complicated relationship with his demanding father, his inner doubts, and how his best friend Max Brod saved his unpublished writings from being burned after his death.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Kafka lived a double life: an insurance lawyer by day and an avant-garde writer by night.
  • His famous story 'Die Verwandlung' tells of Gregor Samsa waking up transformed into a giant insect.
  • His difficult relationship with his father deeply shaped his themes of guilt, alienation, and bureaucracy.
  • Before dying of tuberculosis at age 40, he asked his friend Max Brod to burn all his manuscripts—a request Brod fortunately ignored.
