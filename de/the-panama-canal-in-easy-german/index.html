The Panama Canal in Easy German

Discover the monumental engineering marvel of the Panama Canal, the 82-kilometer waterway that connects the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans across Central America. Explore how this shortcut eliminated the hazardous 15,000-kilometer voyage around Cape Horn, overcoming dense jungles, tropical diseases, and mountainous terrain through an ingenious system of water locks.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • The canal shortens the sea voyage between New York and San Francisco by over 13,000 kilometers.
  • France began construction in 1881 but failed due to financial collapse and rampant tropical diseases.
  • The United States completed the project in 1914 after eradicating mosquito-borne yellow fever and malaria.
  • Massive water locks lift ships 26 meters above sea level to cross Gatun Lake before lowering them back down.
