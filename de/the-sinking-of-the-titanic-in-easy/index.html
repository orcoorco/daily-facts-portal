The Sinking of the Titanic in Easy German

Relive the fateful maiden voyage of the RMS Titanic in April 1912. Celebrated as the largest and most luxurious passenger liner of its day, the "unsinkable" ship met catastrophe in the icy North Atlantic. Learn how an iceberg collision, high speed, and a critical shortage of lifeboats led to one of history's most famous maritime disasters, prompting lasting global safety regulations.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • The Titanic set sail from Southampton to New York on its maiden voyage on April 10, 1912.
  • It struck an iceberg late on April 14 in freezing waters 600 kilometers south of Newfoundland.
  • The ship carried lifeboats for only about half of the people on board due to outdated safety laws.
  • The disaster resulted in mandatory 24-hour radio watches and lifeboats for 100% of passengers.
