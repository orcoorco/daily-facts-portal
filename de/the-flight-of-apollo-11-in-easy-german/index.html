💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Apollo 11 launched on July 16, 1969, powered by the giant 110-meter Saturn V rocket.
  • On July 20, 1969, Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed the lunar module 'Eagle' on the Sea of Tranquility.
  • Neil Armstrong became the first human to walk on the Moon, uttering his immortal words.
  • The astronauts spent over two hours on the lunar surface, collecting 21.5 kg of Moon rocks.
