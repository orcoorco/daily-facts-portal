The History of Coffee in Easy German

Trace the aromatic global journey of coffee, from wild shrub to the world's favorite morning beverage! Discover the legendary Ethiopian story of Kaldi and his dancing goats discovering caffeine. Follow coffee's spread across Yemeni monasteries and bustling Ottoman coffeehouses into 17th-century Europe, where coffeehouses became lively centers for news, commerce, and scientific revolution.

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • Legend tells of an Ethiopian goat herder named Kaldi who noticed his goats energized after eating red berries.
  • Yemeni monks in the 15th century roasted and brewed coffee beans to stay awake during nightly prayers.
  • The first public coffeehouses opened in Istanbul in the 16th century as centers for social conversation.
  • European coffeehouses in London, Paris, and Vienna fostered political debates and stock exchanges.
