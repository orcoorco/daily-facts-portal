The 1936 Summer Olympics in Easy German

Examine the tense, contradictory world of the 1936 Berlin Summer Olympics (Olympische Sommerspiele 1936). Orchestrated by Adolf Hitler and the Nazi regime, the games were staged as a massive propaganda spectacle of peace and modernity. Explore how African American track superstar Jesse Owens shattered Nazi racial myths by winning four gold medals, how the Olympic torch relay was invented for the games, and how Berlin hosted the world's very first live television broadcast.

Spotify Apple Podcasts Substack

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • The Nazi regime invested 42 million Reichsmark in a colossal 100,000-seat stadium and whitewashed anti-Semitic signage.
  • African American athlete Jesse Owens became the undeniable star of the games, winning four historic Olympic gold medals.
  • The modern tradition of the Olympic torch relay from Greece was introduced for the very first time at the 1936 Berlin Games.
  • The Berlin Games featured the world's first live television coverage, broadcast to viewing booths across the city.
