The Real Story of the Ghost Ship Mary Celeste in Easy German

Unravel the maritime world's most enduring mystery: the ghost ship Mary Celeste. In December 1872, the American brigantine was spotted drifting seaworthy under partial sail near the Azores. When rescuers boarded, they found the ship fully stocked with food and water and its cargo of industrial alcohol intact—but Captain Briggs, his family, and the entire crew had vanished without a trace into the Atlantic.

💡 Key Topics & Highlights

  • The Mary Celeste set sail from New York to Genoa in November 1872 carrying 1,700 barrels of alcohol.
  • The British ship Dei Gratia found it drifting in the Atlantic with nobody aboard on December 4, 1872.
  • The vessel was in good condition with plenty of provisions and cargo untouched, but the lifeboat was gone.
  • Leading theories suggest expanding alcohol fumes caused a false panic, leading the crew to abandon ship in haste.
